The project proposal and analysis will be added as the project develops.

## Research orientation (to be confirmed)

- **Origin:** When does the first negative link appear? Is the pair negative from its very first interaction, does negativity emerge after previous non-negative links, or is it a response to a negative link in the opposite direction?
- **Evolution:** Does an initial negative link lead to reciprocity, retaliation, or sustained one-sided hostility?
- **Propagation and broader impact:** Once negativity appears between two communities, does the target subsequently receive more attention from other communities? Is that attention more negative or non-negative, and does overall interaction increase?
