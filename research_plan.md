# Research plan and team meeting

## Shared question

How does negative attention emerge between subreddits, develop within a pair, and relate to attention from other communities? The three analyses should form one data story: **origin → evolution → wider attention**.

## Work split

Each group prepares one clearly structured research notebook, including its question, definitions, methods, initial results, limitations, and a small number of useful figures.

| Research line | Owners | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Origin | Edoardo and Clément | Identify where negativity first appears in an *observed* pair history: on the first recorded link or after earlier non-negative links. For each direction, check whether its first negative link follows a negative link in the other direction. |
| 2. Evolution | Romain | Follow the pair after a negative link: whether and when the target links back, the sentiment of that response, and whether negativity fades, remains one-sided, or becomes mutual. |
| 3. Wider attention | Anaki and Hippolyte | Examine links from third communities after a negative interaction: changes in volume, number of distinct sources, and negative share; explore whether existing network connections or triangles help explain the patterns. |

The first recorded link is not necessarily the first interaction ever. The dataset labels links as **negative** or **non-negative** (neutral and positive combined). Changes after an event are associations, not evidence that the event caused them. These definitions should be consistent across notebooks.

## Thursday morning meeting agenda
